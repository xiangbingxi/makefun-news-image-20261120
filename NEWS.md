# The 36 stories — sources

Every line below was checked against reporting published on Friday
20 November 2026. Figures are the figures the cited outlet reported that
day; where outlets differed (e.g. Kyiv's death toll rising through the day),
the page uses the later, updated figure and says so.

## Featured

1. **UKRAINE — Missiles smash three Kyiv bridges.** Russian ballistic
   missiles hit the Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi bridges
   over the Dnipro; 6 killed in Kyiv, 8 across Ukraine, ~15–17 injured; three
   bridges closed or restricted. *Reuters (via DZRH/Dubai Eye), BBC,
   Ukrainska Pravda.* The overnight barrage also hit a Kharkiv emergency
   hospital (4 injured) and sites in Dnipropetrovsk and Zaporizhzhia.
2. **INDIA — Cyclone Akvan slams India's east coast.** Landfall near
   Gopalpur, Odisha, winds 70–90+ km/h; 300,000+ people evacuated; the third
   cyclone to hit India in under a month. *Reuters, ARN News Centre.*
3. **MARKETS — Asia's AI sell-off deepens.** Nikkei −5.1% (lowest since
   April), Taiwan −6.3% (week −17.5%), Kospi −5.2%, Hang Seng −1.9%,
   Shanghai −1.2%, ASX −1.6%. Anthropic's record $81bn IPO closed +17% at
   $97.17 on day one, then fell ~10–14% on Friday. Dow −1,101 points (−2.2%),
   S&P 500 −2.0%, Nasdaq −2.3% on Thursday. *Bloomberg (via International
   Intrigue), FXStreet, Morningstar/Alliance News, CNBC (via JS Finance
   Daily), TechCrunch.*
4. **CLIMATE — COP31 ends in deadlock.** Final scheduled day in Antalya,
   Türkiye: the draft cover decision drops any fossil-fuel phase-out and the
   $120bn-a-year adaptation finance target; talks spill into overtime; Panama
   vows to block a deal with no fossil-fuel language; civil society calls it
   the "worst COP ever". The US is absent for a second year. *Reuters, CNA,
   UN News, Carbon Brief.*
